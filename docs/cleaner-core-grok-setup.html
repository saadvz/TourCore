Tour Core · For hosts

Set up your cleaning assistant in Grok

Cleaner Core texts your cleaner for you. It offers them each cleaning, sends reminders, and gives them the door code once you say the guests have left. On Grok it comes as a ready-made bot, so you add the bot, answer its questions, and you're done.

You don't need any technical skills. Follow the steps in order, and the bot does most of the work.

About 15 minutes 4 steps Grok sets up the alerts for you

Stuck at any point? That's normal. Two easy ways out:

1. In your Cleaner Core bot chat, type "I'm stuck on step 2" (or whichever step) and describe what you see. The bot will walk you through it.

2. Or text Saad the step number and what the screen says. A screenshot helps, as long as it doesn't show your door code or a secret code.

A few words you'll see

Grok's screens use some technical words. Here's what they mean. You don't need to understand more than this.

Bot
Your own copy of Grok, set up just for running your cleanings. It has its own chat.
Secure card
A special box that appears in the chat for anything secret, like your door code. What you type there is kept private.
Routines
Little helpers the bot makes for itself. One checks your bookings, the other wakes the bot when your cleaner texts.
Webhook URL and key
Two codes Grok shows you near the end. You copy each one into a secure card. That's all.

3

Answer the bot's questions

Bot chat
  1. The bot asks one question at a time. When it shows buttons, tap one. Otherwise answer in your own words. It will ask about:
    1. What you want help with. Pick scheduling cleaners.
    2. Your property: its name, address, and usual checkout and check-in times.
    3. Where your bookings are (Guesty, Google Calendar, Airbnb and so on).
    4. Who's involved: your mobile number and your cleaner's number. Two numbers set up a group chat with your cleaner. If you only have one number, give it as the cleaner's number too, and you'll get a chat with the assistant.
    5. Timing: how early to offer each cleaning, when to remind, and hours not to text.
    6. Your lock, then your door code.
    7. Your cleaning checklist and anything else you prefer.
  2. For your door code (and a calendar link, if it asks), the bot shows a secure card in the chat. Type it in the card and submit it. Don't type it as a normal message.
  3. If your bookings are on a Google calendar, the bot may ask you to connect Google Calendar. Follow its link and sign in to Google.

Not sure how to answer? Just say so. "I don't know, what do most hosts pick?" works fine.

4

Connect the alerts and turn it on

Routine Bot chat

After your last answer, the bot makes its two routines by itself. One of them needs two codes from you, so the bot knows when your cleaner texts.

  1. The bot sends two links, each with a secure card under it. The first is for the Webhook URL, the second for the Webhook key.
  2. Click the first link. It opens the Webhook URL. Copy it, come back, and paste it in the first secure card.
  3. Click the second link. It opens the Webhook key. Copy it, come back, and paste it in the second secure card.
  4. The bot posts "Cleaner Core alerts are connected." That means it works.
  5. The bot shows a summary of everything you told it. If it all looks right, type Activate.
  6. The bot asks about times for your next booking. Say "use my usual times", or give the times for that booking.
  7. Your cleaner gets one text: "Reply YES to get cleaning texts, or STOP to opt out." You might let them know it's coming.

Use the two links the bot sends. Opening the Routines page yourself won't show the Webhook URL.

You're done! 🎉 That's the whole setup.

What happens from now on

Most days you don't need to do anything. The bot posts in its chat when something needs you.

The bot posts a short summary in its chat right away, like "Maria says she'll arrive at 11:30."

Cleaning finished

If something goes wrong

Click a problem to see what to do.

It says my invite code "isn't right", "has expired", or "was already used"

Each invite code works only once, and only for 7 days.

What to do: text Saad for a new code, then say "Set up Cleaner Core" again.

The bot link doesn't open, or there's no "Add to Grok Bot" button

You may not be signed in, or your account can't add bots yet.

What to do: sign in to Grok and open the link again. If the button still isn't there, text Saad.

I can't find the Webhook URL

It only shows when you open it from the link the bot sent.

What to do: type "Send me the webhook links again" in the bot's chat.

The bot never posts "Cleaner Core alerts are connected."

What to do: tell the bot "the alerts didn't connect". It will check and walk you through it.

Setup is finished but my cleaner never got a text

Usually the times for the next booking haven't been confirmed yet.

What to do: tell the bot "confirm the times for the next booking". If it says nothing was sent, text Saad.

Anything else

What to do: type "I'm stuck" in the bot's chat and describe what you see, or text Saad the step number and what the screen says.

When you ask Saad or anyone else for help, never send your door code, your invite code, or the two webhook codes.
