Set up your cleaning assistant in ChatGPT

Cleaner Core texts your cleaner for you. It offers them each cleaning, sends reminders, and gives them the door code once you say the guests have left. You connect it to ChatGPT one time, and after that you just chat with ChatGPT.

You don't need any technical skills. Follow the steps in order, and copy and paste when the guide tells you to.

About 15 minutes 4 steps Needs a computer (not the phone app)

Stuck at any point? That's normal. Two easy ways out:

1. In your ChatGPT chat, type "I'm stuck on step 2" (or whichever step) and describe what you see. ChatGPT will walk you through it.

2. Or text Saad the step number and what the screen says. A screenshot helps, as long as it doesn't show your door code or a secret code.

Before you start

Have these ready. If one is missing, a step will stop partway through.

Your ChatGPT account

ChatGPT Plus or higher

The free plan can't add Cleaner Core. Plus has been tested and works.

From Saad

Your invite code

Saad sends it to you. It only works once, and only for 7 days, so do the setup soon after you get it.

You type these in step 3

Two phone numbers

Your mobile number and your cleaner's. With both, you get a group chat with your cleaner. Only have one number? Enter it as the cleaner's number too, and you get a chat with the assistant instead.

Your own details

Property basics at hand

Your property's address, usual checkout and check-in times, your cleaner's name, and your door code.

A few words you'll see

ChatGPT's screens use some technical words. Here's what they mean. You don't need to understand more than this.

Developer Mode
Sounds technical, but it's just a switch that lets you add Cleaner Core. You turn it on once.
Connector (or MCP server)
An add-on that lets ChatGPT talk to Cleaner Core. You add it once.
Scheduled task
A helper ChatGPT makes for itself. It checks your bookings and your cleaner's messages every hour.
Private link
A one-time web page from Cleaner Core where you type anything secret, like your door code. It's safer than typing it in the chat.

The four steps

Tick "Done" as you finish each step. The ticks are remembered in this browser, so you can take a break and come back.

1

Turn on Developer Mode

ChatGPT settings
  1. On your computer, go to chatgpt.com and sign in.
  2. Click your name or picture in the corner, then Settings.
  3. Click Apps & Connectors. On some accounts it's just called Connectors.
  4. Click Advanced, and switch Developer Mode on.

If you use ChatGPT through work, your admin may need to turn this on. Ask Saad if you can't find it.

2

Add Cleaner Core to ChatGPT

ChatGPT settings
  1. Still in Apps & Connectors, click the button to add or create a new one. If it asks what kind, pick custom MCP server (not a plugin).
  2. In Name, type Cleaner Core.
  3. In the box that asks for a URL (a web address), paste this. Click Copy, then click in the box and paste:
    https://tourcore-production-8b80.up.railway.app/mcp/cleaner-core
  4. For Authentication, pick OAuth. Leave everything else as it is.
  5. Click Create (it may say Connect).
  6. A page titled Connect ChatGPT to Cleaner Core opens. Type the invite code Saad sent you, then click Allow.

Only if your bookings are on a Google calendar: in the same list, find Google Calendar and connect it. If you use Guesty or Airbnb, skip this.

Only add Cleaner Core once. If you're not sure whether it worked, ask Saad before you try again.

3

Answer ChatGPT's questions

Chat with ChatGPT Private link
  1. Start a new chat.
  2. Click the + button next to where you type and add Cleaner Core to the chat. ChatGPT can only use it once it's added.
  3. Type Set up Cleaner Core and press Enter.
  4. ChatGPT asks one question at a time. When it shows buttons, click one. Otherwise answer in your own words. It will ask about:
    1. What you want help with. Pick scheduling cleaners.
    2. Your property: its name, address, and usual checkout and check-in times.
    3. Where your bookings are (Guesty, Google Calendar, Airbnb and so on).
    4. Who's involved: your mobile number and your cleaner's number. Two numbers set up a group chat with your cleaner. If you only have one number, give it as the cleaner's number too, and you'll get a chat with the assistant.
    5. Timing: how early to offer each cleaning, when to remind, and hours not to text.
    6. Your lock, then your door code.
    7. Supplies, photos, your checklist, and anything else you prefer.
  5. For your door code (and a calendar link, if it asks), ChatGPT sends a private link. Click it, type the code there, click Save, come back and type "saved".

ChatGPT asks you to approve something? If it matches what you just asked for, click approve.

Not sure how to answer? Just say so. "I don't know, what do most hosts pick?" works fine.

4

Check the hourly task and turn it on

Scheduled tasks Chat with ChatGPT

After your last answer, ChatGPT makes a scheduled task called Cleaner Core by itself. You don't have to copy anything.

  1. When ChatGPT says the task is made, open ChatGPT's scheduled tasks page (ChatGPT will tell you where) and check:
    1. There is exactly one task called Cleaner Core.
    2. It runs every hour, and it's on.
  2. Go back to your setup chat. ChatGPT shows a summary of everything you told it. If it all looks right, type Activate.
  3. ChatGPT asks about times for your bookings. Say "use my usual times", or set them one at a time.
  4. Your cleaner gets one text asking them to reply YES. You might let them know it's coming.

Until you activate, each hourly run just says "Nothing new." That's normal.

You're done! 🎉 That's the whole setup.

What happens from now on

ChatGPT checks in once an hour. For anything urgent, your group text with the cleaner is the fastest way to see it.

Group text

Your cleaner texts

You see their message right away in the group text on your phone. ChatGPT also picks it up at its next hourly check.

New chat

Reply through ChatGPT

Open a chat, add Cleaner Core with +, and say "Maria texted, draft a reply". It sends only after you say yes.

Door code

The code waits for your yes

Tell ChatGPT "the guests have left". Only then does your cleaner get the door code.

Scheduled tasks

See what happened

Open the Cleaner Core task's latest run to see what came in overnight.

Remember: in every new chat, click + and add Cleaner Core first. Otherwise ChatGPT won't know about your cleanings.

If something goes wrong

Click a problem to see what to do. These all came up while testing, so you're not the first.

It says my invite code "isn't right", "has expired", or "was already used"

Each invite code works only once, and only for 7 days.

What to do: text Saad for a new code, then click Connect on Cleaner Core again.

I can't find Developer Mode

It's only on chatgpt.com in a web browser, not in the phone or desktop app. On a free plan it isn't there at all.

What to do: open chatgpt.com on a computer. If it's still missing, text Saad.

"This tool call was blocked by OpenAI's safety checks"

ChatGPT's own safety check stopped it. It's usually a false alarm.

What to do: type "try again". It normally goes through.

ChatGPT asks what Cleaner Core is, or gives a general answer

Cleaner Core isn't added to this chat.

What to do: click + and add Cleaner Core, or start a new chat with it added.

A private link says it has expired

Private links stop working after 10 minutes, or once they've been used.

What to do: ask ChatGPT "Can I have a new link?"

The Cleaner Core task is paused, or ChatGPT warns it hasn't checked in

What to do: turn it back on from the scheduled tasks page. If it keeps pausing, delete it and ask ChatGPT to "make the Cleaner Core task again".

Setup is finished but my cleaner never got a text

What to do: tell ChatGPT "confirm the times for the next booking". If it says "Nothing was sent yet", text Saad.

Anything else

What to do: type "I'm stuck" in the chat and describe what you see, or text Saad the step number and what the screen says.

When you ask Saad or anyone else for help, never send your door code or your invite code.
