Tour Core · For hosts

Set up your cleaning assistant in Muse

Cleaner Core texts your cleaner for you. It offers them each cleaning, sends reminders, and gives them the door code once you say the guests have left. In Muse, you paste one message and Muse does most of the setup itself.

You don't need any technical skills. Follow the steps in order, and type in the boxes Muse hands you.

About 15 minutes 4 steps Starts with one paste

Stuck at any point? That's normal. Two easy ways out:

1. In your Muse chat, type "I'm stuck on step 2" (or whichever step) and describe what you see. Muse will walk you through it.

2. Or text Saad the step number and what the screen says. A screenshot helps, as long as it doesn't show your door code or a secret code.

A few words you'll see

Muse's screens use some technical words. Here's what they mean. You don't need to understand more than this.

Connector
An add-on that lets Muse talk to Cleaner Core. Muse adds it for you.
Choice card
Buttons Muse shows for a question. Tap one to answer.
Muse's browser
A web page Muse opens and then hands to you. You type in it yourself. This is how your door code stays private.
Recurring task
A helper Muse makes that checks your bookings and your cleaner's messages on a schedule you pick.

The four steps

Tick "Done" as you finish each step. The ticks are remembered in this browser, so you can take a break and come back.

2

Connect with your invite code

Muse's browser Muse settings
  1. Muse adds the Cleaner Core connector, then shows you a page titled Connect Muse to Cleaner Core. It may open in Muse's browser and be handed to you.
  2. Type the invite code Saad sent you, then click Allow yourself. Muse isn't allowed to do this part for you.
  3. Muse then asks you to change one setting. Open Settings → Connectors → Cleaner Core and let Muse use it without asking each time. This lets the checks run while you're away.
  4. Only if your bookings are on a Google calendar: connect Muse's Google Calendar connector too, in the same Connectors list.

Never type your invite code in the chat or in a choice card. It only goes on the Cleaner Core page.

If Muse can't add the connector itself, it will ask you to add it in Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector. Name it Cleaner Core and use this address: https://tourcore-production-8b80.up.railway.app/mcp/cleaner-core

3

Answer Muse's questions

Muse chat Muse's browser
  1. Muse asks one question at a time. When it shows a choice card, tap an answer. Otherwise answer in your own words. It will ask about:
    1. What you want help with. Pick scheduling cleaners.
    2. Your property: its name, address, and usual checkout and check-in times.
    3. Where your bookings are (Guesty, Google Calendar, Airbnb and so on).
    4. Who's involved: your mobile number and your cleaner's number. Two numbers set up a group chat with your cleaner. If you only have one number, give it as the cleaner's number too, and you'll get a chat with the assistant.
    5. Timing: how early to offer each cleaning, when to remind, and hours not to text.
    6. Your lock, then your door code.
    7. Your cleaning checklist and anything else you prefer.
  2. For your door code, Muse opens a private Cleaner Core page in its browser and hands it to you. Type the code there and click Save. Muse doesn't see what you type.
  3. For a calendar link, Muse gives you two choices:
    1. I have the link: paste it in the page Muse hands you, then click Save.
    2. Find it for me: Muse opens your booking site (like Airbnb). When it asks you to sign in, you type your own password there. Muse then finds the link and saves it.

Not sure how to answer? Just say so. "I don't know, what do most hosts pick?" works fine.

4

Pick how often to check, then turn it on

Muse chat
  1. Muse asks how often to check for new bookings and messages. Pick one:
    Every minuteFastest. Uses the most of your Muse usage.
    Every 5 minutesQuick, with less usage.
    Every 15 minutesA good middle ground.
    Every hourUses the least. Slowest to notice things.
    You can ask Muse to change this any time.
  2. Muse makes a recurring task called Cleaner Core by itself.
  3. Muse shows a summary of everything you told it. If it all looks right, type Activate.
  4. Muse asks about times for your bookings. Say "use my usual times", or set them one at a time.
  5. Your cleaner gets one text asking them to reply YES. You might let them know it's coming.

You're done! 🎉 That's the whole setup.

What happens from now on

Muse checks in on the schedule you picked. For anything urgent, your group text with the cleaner is the fastest way to see it.

Group text

Your cleaner texts

You see their message right away in the group text on your phone. Muse picks it up at its next check.

Muse chat

Reply through Muse

Tell Muse "Maria texted, draft a reply". It sends only after you say yes.

Door code

The code waits for your yes

Tell Muse "the guests have left". Only then does your cleaner get the door code.

Summary

Cleaning finished

Muse looks at the cleaner's photos, checks them against your checklist, and sends you a short report.

If something goes wrong

Click a problem to see what to do.

It says my invite code "isn't right", "has expired", or "was already used"

Each invite code works only once, and only for 7 days.

What to do: text Saad for a new code, then tell Muse "connect Cleaner Core again".

Muse asks me to type a door code, password or link in the chat

It shouldn't. Secrets only go on the page Muse hands you.

What to do: don't type it. Reply "please open the private page in your browser for me instead".

A private page says it has expired

Private pages stop working after 10 minutes, or once they've been used.

What to do: ask Muse "Can I have a new page?"

The checks stopped, or Muse warns it hasn't checked in

Usually Muse is waiting for an approval nobody saw.

What to do: check Settings → Connectors → Cleaner Core is set to not ask each time. Then ask Muse to turn the Cleaner Core task back on.

Muse is using more of my usage than I'd like

What to do: tell Muse "check less often, every hour".

Setup is finished but my cleaner never got a text

What to do: tell Muse "confirm the times for the next booking". If it says nothing was sent, text Saad.

Anything else

What to do: type "I'm stuck" in the chat and describe what you see, or text Saad the step number and what the screen says.

When you ask Saad or anyone else for help, never send your door code, your invite code, or a booking-site password.
